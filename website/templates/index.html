Pool water — Waiting for a reading… Outdoor air — Waiting for a reading…

A small experiment in pretending we can predict the pool.

The water reading comes from a DS18B20 strapped to the filter return PVC. Outdoor air is whichever of two neighborhood Weather Underground stations answers first. None of this is an official anything — it's a sandbox for figuring out whether Monte Sano really does run ten degrees cooler than Huntsville (it does, sometimes) and whether we can forecast the pool from the air (we're going to try).
